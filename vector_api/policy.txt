Nova E-commerce accepts returns for most items within 30 days of the delivery date. To be eligible for a return, the item must be in its original condition, unused, and include all original packaging and accessories.

Once we receive and inspect your returned item, refunds are processed within 5 to 7 business days. The refunded amount will be credited back to your original payment method. Shipping costs are non-refundable unless the return is due to a defective or incorrect product.

Standard shipping typically takes 3 to 5 business days, while expedited shipping takes 1 to 2 business days. Orders are processed Monday through Friday, excluding major holidays. You will receive a tracking number via email once your order has shipped.

Currently, Nova E-commerce ships to addresses within the United States, Canada, and the United Kingdom. We do not offer global shipping at this time. International customers are responsible for any applicable customs duties or import taxes.

All electronic products, including monitors, soundbars, and peripherals, come with a standard 1-year manufacturer warranty. This warranty covers hardware defects and internal malfunctions under normal use. It does not cover accidental physical damage, water damage, or unauthorized modifications.

Warranty and Accidental Damage Policy:
All electronics and monitors include a 12 to 36-month standard manufacturer warranty covering hardware defects, dead pixels, and internal circuitry failures. However, accidental physical damage, liquid spills, water damage, and dropped items are strictly excluded from standard warranty coverage. Customers with water-damaged items may request a paid out-of-warranty repair quote through customer support.
